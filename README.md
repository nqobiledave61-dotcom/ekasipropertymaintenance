## 📖 Project Overview
This project involves designing and developing a responsive website for **Ekasi Property Maintenance (EPM)**, a small business specializing in handyman services, lawn care, plumbing, and home renovations. The website will serve as a professional portfolio, improve customer engagement, and provide an online platform for requesting quotes.

## 🎯 Website Goals and Objectives
- Establish a strong online presence for EPM  
- Showcase completed projects through a gallery  
- Provide a simple, user-friendly interface accessible to all age groups  
- Enable customers to request quotes directly online  
- Build credibility with banks, investors, and homeowners’ associations  

## ⚙️ Key Features and Functionality
- **Homepage:** Introduction to EPM and services offered  
- **Gallery:** High-quality images of completed projects  
- **About Us:** Company history, mission, and values  
- **Contact Us:** Contact form and business details  
- **Get a Quote:** Interactive form for instant service requests  
- **Responsive Design:** Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices  

## 🗓️ Timeline and Milestones
- **Week 1–2:** Project proposal, sitemap, and design plan  
- **Week 3–4:** Develop homepage, gallery, and about us pages  
- **Week 5–6:** Implement contact form and quote request functionality  
- **Week 7:** Testing, debugging, and responsive adjustments  
- **Week 8:** Final deployment and documentation
